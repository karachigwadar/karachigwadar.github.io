About KGI Logistics

Your Trusted Partner in Global Logistics

Karachi Gwadar International Logistics (Pvt) Ltd. is a premier freight forwarding company established in 2010, headquartered in Karachi — Pakistan's commercial hub and gateway to international trade.

We specialize in sea freight, air freight, land transport, project cargo, warehousing, and customs clearance services across 80+ countries, serving importers, exporters, and businesses of all sizes.
