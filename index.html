How Chargeable Weight Works

Airlines charge the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight:

Volumetric weight (kg) = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000

Example: a 100 × 80 × 60 cm carton = 80 kg volumetric, even if it weighs less. We measure at pickup and advise smarter packing to cut your air bill.

Service Overview

Clearance Without the Headaches

Customs is where shipments are won or lost. As licensed customs brokers, KGI’s clearance team files import and export Goods Declarations through Pakistan’s WeBOC system and the Pakistan Single Window (PSW), classifies goods under the correct HS codes and clears cargo at Karachi Port, Port Qasim, Jinnah International Airport and upcountry dry ports.

We work proactively: documents are checked and duty/tax estimates confirmed before the vessel or flight arrives, so examination, assessment and out-of-charge happen without idle days. Good to Know

Pakistan Single Window (PSW) is the national electronic portal connecting traders, banks, customs and regulatory bodies for import/export filings, while WeBOC is Pakistan Customs’ computerized clearance system where your Goods Declaration is processed.

Our team lodges and tracks every declaration in both systems — you receive status updates without having to learn either platform.
